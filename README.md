## Featured Projects


| Project | Key Finding | Practical Applications |
|:--------|:------------|:-----------------------|
| [Kenyans Investor Segmentation](https://github.com/AlexiaLukangu/behavioural-finance-analysis) | Identified gender-based behavioural finance biases among NSE investors, revealing higher self-reported risk tolerance among female investors, familiarity bias among male investors, and herding behaviour among female investors. | Supports customer segmentation, personalised investment advisory services, behavioural risk profiling, and financial literacy programmes. |
| [Crypto Market Intelligence](https://github.com/AlexiaLukangu/behavioural-finance-analysis) | Demonstrated that market sentiment explained **10.2%** of the variation in stablecoin trading volumes and established a measurable relationship between the Crypto Fear & Greed Index and USDT/USDC trading activity. | Supports market monitoring, investment decision-making, portfolio risk assessment, and data-driven trading strategies within digital asset markets. |
| [African Development Indicator Intelligence Dataset](https://github.com/AlexiaLukangu/behavioural-finance-analysis) |Revealed that **79%** of African development indicators originate from non-African institutions, highlighting structural dependencies in development measurement and governance. | Provides a reusable dataset for policy analysis, improves transparency in development research, and supports evidence-based decision-making for researchers and policymakers. |
